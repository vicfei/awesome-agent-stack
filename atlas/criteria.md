# Status rubric (long form)

- 🟢 **production** — at least one publicly verifiable production deployment + ≥6 months of maintenance + a track record of managed breaking changes (or a stability promise).
- 🟡 **beta** — real usage beyond the authors, active development, rough edges acknowledged.
- ⚪ **experimental** — pre-adoption, explicitly unstable, or research-grade.

Status describes the *project's* maturity, not its importance — an experimental entry can be the most interesting thing in a layer.

## governance-adjacent flag

Marked ✓ when the entry itself ships at least one of: an admission/permission policy surface, tamper-evident evidence (hash-chained logs, attestations), or a first-class human-approval mechanism. An SDK that *could be used to build* one does not qualify — it has to ship it.
